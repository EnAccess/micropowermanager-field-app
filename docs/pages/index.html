Privacy Policy

MPM Field App · Published by the EnAccess Foundation · Effective 9 July 2026

The MPM Field App is a mobile application for field agents of energy service providers that use MicroPowerManager to manage mini-grid customers, meters, and payments. The app is free and open source; its complete source code is available at github.com/EnAccess/micropowermanager-field-app.

This policy explains what data the app handles, where that data goes, and who is responsible for it.

The short version

  • The app sends data only to the MicroPowerManager server that you or your organization select — nowhere else.
  • If your organization uses the MicroPowerManager Cloud, EnAccess hosts that data on your organization’s behalf; if your organization runs its own server, EnAccess never receives any data.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • Your organization (the energy service provider you work for) controls the data and is responsible for it.

Data the app handles

The app is a data-collection tool operated by trained field agents. In the course of normal use it handles:

  • Agent account data — the email address and password you use to sign in to your organization’s MicroPowerManager server.
  • Customer records — names, phone numbers, addresses, and registration questionnaire answers that agents enter when registering customers.
  • Location data — the device’s GPS position, used to geotag customers and meters registered in the field. Location is captured only while the app is in use.
  • Photos and documents — pictures taken with the camera or selected from the photo library, used to attach documents such as signed contracts to a customer record.
  • Payment records — payment and transaction entries recorded for customers.

Where the data goes

All data entered in the app is transmitted over HTTPS to exactly one destination: the MicroPowerManager server selected at sign-in. This is either the MicroPowerManager Cloud service, the public demo server, or a server hosted by your own organization.

In all cases, your organization determines how the data is used, how long it is retained, and who can access it, and remains responsible for it as the data controller.

EnAccess operates the MicroPowerManager Cloud and the demo server. When your organization uses one of these, EnAccess stores the collected data on your organization’s behalf, acting as a data processor, and does not use it for any purpose other than providing the service. When your organization hosts its own MicroPowerManager server, EnAccess has no access to the data at all.

Data stored on the device

The app is designed to work offline. Records you create without an internet connection are stored on the device and uploaded automatically when connectivity returns. Reference data (such as city lists) is cached locally to speed up the app. Sign-in credentials are stored in the operating system’s encrypted secure storage. All locally stored data is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Third-party services

When you open the map to pick a location, the app loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and the Leaflet map library from the unpkg.com content delivery network. Those services receive your device’s IP address and the map area being viewed, as is inherent to loading any web content. No other third-party service is contacted, and no data is sold or shared for advertising or marketing purposes.

App permissions

Permission Why the app asks for it
Location To geotag customers and meters you register in the field.
Camera To capture customer documents, such as signed contracts, and attach them to a customer record.
Photos To attach an existing photo of a document to a customer record.

Each permission is requested only when the corresponding feature is first used, and the app remains usable if a permission is declined.

Your rights and data deletion

Because customer data is held by the energy service provider operating the MicroPowerManager server, requests to access, correct, or delete personal data should be directed to that provider. If you are a customer, contact your energy service provider. If you are a field agent, contact your employer. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the device itself.

Children

The app is a professional tool for field agents and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. The history of this document is publicly available in the app’s source repository.

Contact

The app is published by the EnAccess Foundation (enaccess.org). For questions about this policy or the app, open an issue at github.com/EnAccess/micropowermanager-field-app/issues or reach EnAccess through the contact options on its website.
